# Colorbox — Cherry Audio Notes

Short module descriptions for the Cherry Audio Notes fields. Versions and module descriptions follow the current Colorbox canonical list.

## RGB — 4.0.3

RGB is a three-stage saturation processor with distinct RED, GREEN, and BLUE stages. Its normalled routing lets you cascade the stages or patch them in parallel and stereo arrangements.

## CMYK — 1.0.3

CMYK is a four-stage wavefolder with bipolar input level controls and fold CV. Use its stages to shape signals from subtle harmonic changes to complex, animated timbres.

## HSB — 1.0.2

HSB contains two independent HUE → SATURATION → BRILLIANCE processors for shaping and coloring audio. Use them separately or combine their stages for layered, evolving tones.
