**Three-stage saturation processor · v4.0.3**

rgb combines three distinct waveshaping stages, each with its own drive control. RED brings firmer, more aggressive saturation; GREEN is rounder and more gradual; BLUE becomes increasingly asymmetric as drive rises, adding an uneven, gritty edge. Together they range from gentle harmonic thickening to dense, layered distortion.

The stages form a normalled RED → GREEN → BLUE cascade, with individual inputs and outputs for independent processing, stereo, or dual-mono patches.

**The DC FILTER switch** enables DC blocking after each saturation stage, removing signal offset before it reaches the next stage or output. Switch it off to retain that offset and explore how it affects subsequent saturation.

**Bypass prioritizes low CPU use.** Switching it on or off can produce pops and clicks because processing stops immediately without a crossfade. Use an external mute when you need a quiet transition—or embrace the rough edges as part of its DIY electronic character.
