# FMC development brief history

The early scratch brief is superseded by the tested module. It proposed a core-only prototype before a Designer panel existed, so its status and wiring questions are obsolete. Its tentative ranges and assumptions were reconciled against the final implementation and are now documented in [Design-Research.md](Design-Research.md), the [developer notes](../DEVELOPER-NOTES.md), and the archived source comments. The original scratch file was not retained because it duplicated unreviewed guesses and incorrectly described the project as unwired.
