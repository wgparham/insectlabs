# N02 1.0.0

First canonical release: independent EMS-inspired analog noise and clocked digital noise, both with colour and level controls, dedicated outputs and an equal-power blend. SPEED spans 20 Hz–48 kHz and automatically selects repeating 7/15/31-bit sequences with hysteresis and short transitions.

Release housekeeping preserves the final panel and hero, sets Notes to `v1.0.0`, and fixes the new bipolar MIX range to follow the D-left/A-right labels across its full travel. Typed MIX and tooltip text follow percent digital. No other voice, filter, timing or gain arithmetic changed. Earlier development candidates and redundant Designer backups are superseded. The generic LFSR step is preserved in DSP primitives; module-specific design evidence remains in this archive.

Canonical; Cherry Audio publication deferred under the series plan.
