# CXL reference archive

These documents informed the CXL design. They are retained as source material, not as a claim that the software reproduces any of the referenced hardware.

- [`emt140.pdf`](emt140.pdf) — supplied EMT 140 service-manual collection, including mechanical, electrical and amplifier information.
- [`EMT_140_ts_1971.pdf`](EMT_140_ts_1971.pdf) — supplied EMT 140 TS product brochure and block-level description.
- [`Dattorro-1997-EffectDesign-Part1.pdf`](Dattorro-1997-EffectDesign-Part1.pdf) — Jon Dattorro, “Effect Design, Part 1: Reverberator and Other Filters,” JAES 45(9), 1997.

Interpretation, page references and design consequences are summarized in [`../RESEARCH.md`](../RESEARCH.md). The original user-supplied scratch notes are in [`../research/Notes Towards A CXL.md`](../research/Notes%20Towards%20A%20CXL.md).
