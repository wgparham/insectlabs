# Teletape Workshop changelog

## 2026-10-22 — FMC 1.0.0

Canonized FMC Four Channel Modulator after the user-authorized SDK, VMOD, DSP and stub-wiring checks passed. Archived the four-voice/four-lane source pair, panel, manual, developer notes, design research, pedal references, tests, and headless performance measurement. The current release fixes the typed-edit callback duplication and keeps the direct lane bypass and half-sum MIX behavior consistent. Fourteen modules are canonical; Cherry Audio publication remains deferred and the full lineup is still open.

## 2026-10-21 — HGD 1.0.0

Canonized HGD High Gain Device after the user confirmed the working candidate builds, loads and passes functional tests. HGD references remain module-specific. Thirteen modules are now canonical; Cherry Audio publication remains deferred until the series lineup is locked.

## 2026-10-20 — FPA 1.0.0

Canonized FPA Four Pre-Amps after the user-authorized signed tone-range correction and passing strict SDK/DSP checks. Tone now follows the existing −1..+1 panel sweep with 0 centered; C/Re/D are neutral at center, while O retains its full logarithmic low-pass sweep. Archived the matched project/source pair, panel artwork, manual, developer notes, research, review and repeatable tests. Roland manual duplicates were removed from FPA working references after matching them byte-for-byte to the existing SM1084 reference archive. Cherry Audio publication remains deferred and the full collection lineup is still open; twelve modules are canonical.

## 2026-10-20 — CED 1.0.0

Canonized CED Chorus Ensemble Device after the matched pair compiled against the installed Voltage SDK and its headless DSP and stub-wiring checks passed. Archived the pair, current panel image, manual, developer notes, review, validation harness and results. The reusable shared chorus core is preserved in DSP primitives, and a deterministic 24-bit audition render is registered in TestBench. Native Designer loading and user listening remain unverified in this closeout; Cherry Audio publication and the final module lineup remain deferred.

## 2026-10-18 — CXL 1.0.0

Canonized CXL Algorithmic Reverberation after the user approved the 8.5-bit extended-character audition. ## 2026-10-17 - OSC 1.0.0

Canonized OSC Primary Oscillator after user approval. Preserved the shared-phase shapes, level/DC interactions, dedicated V/oct input and through-zero linear FM. Reusable waveform helpers are retained in DSP primitives; obsolete working copies and comparison pairs are removed after archive checks. Nine modules are now canonical; publication remains deferred.

## 2026-10-16 — N02 1.0.0

Canonized N02 Dual Noise Generator after user approval and final checks. Archived the final pair and hero, manual, developer notes, design evidence, tests, review and hashes. Release housekeeping fixes the new bipolar MIX range and direction to follow the D-left/A-right panel labels. Preserved the reusable LFSR step in DSP primitives and removed superseded working duplicates and Designer backups after archive verification.

Eight modules are now canonical, including b08. The final series lineup remains open; Cherry Audio publication awaits the complete collection. Previous module history remains in each module's release notes and review.
