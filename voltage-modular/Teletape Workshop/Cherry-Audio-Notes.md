# Teletape Workshop — Cherry Audio Notes

Short module descriptions for the Cherry Audio Notes fields. Versions and descriptions follow the current Teletape Workshop canonical list.

## RVG Random Voltage Generator — 2.0.0

RVG creates two independent normalized-tanh-shaped stepped random voltages with variable event timing, manual and external reselection, and a +4 V trigger output. Set the three-position hold switch for clean compensated output, gentle factory drift, or stronger failure-style droop.

## b08 Blanking Panel — 1.0.0

b08 is an 8 HP visual blanking panel for filling unused rack space, with Teletape Workshop branding and tape-reel artwork. It has no custom DSP or functional controls.

## TSL Triple Slew Limiter — 1.0.0

Three independent slew channels with exponential manual rates from 0.05 to 1000 V/s. Channels 1 and 2 offer rate CV; channel 3 adds gated track-and-hold. Turn clockwise for faster response. T&H tracks above 3 V and holds at or below 3 V. Useful for pitch glide, CV shaping and waveform slope limiting.
