# SVF 1.0.0 — 2026-10-15

- Canonizes the approved dual Synthi 100-derived state-variable filter.
- Provides simultaneous HIGH, BAND and LOW outputs per channel.
- Adds independent, SERIES and PARALLEL routing plus selectable first-core tap for the series feed.
- Preserves the approved 5–15000 Hz manual frequency range, 0.5 V/octave full-depth CV, resonance response, panel layout and user-saved defaults.
- Includes the nonlinear 96 kHz core and established 2× HalfBand19 resampling path.
- Cherry Audio publication remains deferred until the complete Teletape collection is ready.
