# OFB 1.0.0 — User manual

Eight fixed octave bands: 63, 125, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000 and 8000 Hz. Connect audio to IN and listen at OUT. Each knob controls its band in the main mix: CCW silent, CW full, logarithmic response. Knobs default full. Labels identify fixed bands; the controls do not retune them. All-full is not unity broadband gain.

Each left CV jack adds to its knob position: +5 V adds one full sweep, negative CV subtracts, bounded to the knob range. Start the knob at zero for a 0–5 V envelope; use negative CV to close a fully raised knob. External CV responds at audio rate; abrupt changes can click or create sidebands. Typed knob entry is 0–100 percent.

Each right jack is the clean pre-level band output, independent of its knob and CV. Main OUT combines the bands with gentle shared-headroom saturation; stronger input or more open bands increase compression. Manual changes smooth over approximately 5 ms. Outputs have approximately 0.1875 ms alignment delay.

Host bypass passes IN directly to OUT and silences band taps. Returning from bypass or restoring controls clears transient filter history. Voltage Modular saves knob positions. Approved 2026-10-14; publication deferred until the complete Teletape series.
