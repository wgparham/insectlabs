# sn46 retained design notes

**Module**

v0.0.1m

a cut down version of sn
16u, for when the whole test bench isn't needed. A whole lot of standard voltages and test tones in 8hp. Let me know if you think that it is particularly missing functions that you think this model should have. But, in my mind this is more than enough voltages and tuning tones to get through most sessions unless I need something like Hz/V conversion, or alternate tuning.

**Voltage**

Sweepable voltage source. 0-12 Volts continuous. Negative values out of the NEGATIVE OUTPUT, and positive values out of the POSITIVE OUTPUT jack simultaneously.

**colophon**

manufacturer colophon

**Negative Output**

NEGATIVE voltage as set by a combination of the FREQUENCY and CENTIVOLTS knobs.

**Positive Output**

POSITIVE voltage as set by a combination of the FREQUENCY and CENTIVOLTS knobs.

**1 Volt Fine Tune**

Sweepable 0-1 V in cV. Added to V voltage at the NEGATIVE and POSITIVE OUTPUTS.

**A Reference Tone**

A440 tone for tuning. If the 432 SWITCH is on, this puts out A432.

**Pink Noise**

Pink Noise output

**Slow Pulses**

Slow Pulses output. The same SLOW PULSES as on sn-16u.

**1 Hz Output**

1 Hz steady tone output

**100 Hz output**

100 Hz tone

**F Sharp Reference Tone**

The F# above the A440/432 tone. When the 432 switch is engaged this F# is scaled to A432

**5 V Output**

5 Volt Output

**9 V Output**

9 V continuous output

**0 V Output**

0 V output

**Volt Meter**

Displays the voltage output at the POSITIVE and NEGATIVE OUTPUT jacks. Four digit displey means, two integers for the Volts, and a decimal point, followed by two decimals for the cV.

F Sharp is below A, as confirmed by the user; the original note saying above was incorrect.
